# References and Regulatory Learning Resources

The following sources support the educational concepts in this repository. Always consult the current version and applicable national/regional requirements before using any reporting procedure.

1. **International Council for Harmonisation (ICH).** ICH E2D(R1): Post-Approval Safety Data: Definitions and Standards for Management and Reporting of Individual Case Safety Reports.  
   https://www.ich.org/page/efficacy-guidelines

2. **World Health Organization – Uppsala Monitoring Centre.** WHO–UMC System for Standardised Case Causality Assessment.  
   https://who-umc.org/causality-assessment/

3. **Pharmacovigilance Programme of India (PvPI), Indian Pharmacopoeia Commission.** National pharmacovigilance information, reporting resources and forms.  
   https://ipc.gov.in/

4. **U.S. Food and Drug Administration.** Postmarketing safety reporting and adverse event reporting resources.  
   https://www.fda.gov/safety

5. **European Medicines Agency.** Good Pharmacovigilance Practices (GVP), including individual case safety report management.  
   https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/human-regulatory-overview/post-authorisation/pharmacovigilance-post-authorisation/good-pharmacovigilance-practices

6. **Council for International Organizations of Medical Sciences (CIOMS).** Pharmacovigilance and safety reporting resources.  
   https://cioms.ch/

## Reference-use notes
- The exact reporting requirements, timelines, seriousness definitions and permitted fields can vary by jurisdiction and product type.
- The four minimum ICSR elements are case-validity criteria, not a complete documentation checklist.
- Patient and reporter information must be handled in authorised, privacy-protective systems.
- This repository is educational and does not replace official forms, regulations or institutional SOPs.
