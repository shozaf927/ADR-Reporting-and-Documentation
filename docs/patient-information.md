# Module 01 — Patient Information

## Purpose
Capture sufficient patient information to support case identification, clinical interpretation and follow-up while protecting confidentiality.

## Information to document
- Patient identifier permitted by the reporting system.
- Age or date of birth, as permitted and necessary.
- Sex, where relevant to the event and reporting requirements.
- Body weight, especially where dose is weight-based or weight affects interpretation.
- Relevant demographic or pregnancy information, when applicable.
- Relevant medical history, allergies and comorbidities (expanded in the clinical-context module).

## Confidentiality and data minimisation
- Use initials only when the applicable form or procedure specifically requests them; initials alone may not satisfy regulatory patient-identifiability requirements.
- Never publish names, addresses, phone numbers, medical-record numbers, exact dates of birth or other identifying patient details in a public GitHub repository.
- Use a fictional or coded identifier in training examples.
- Store identifiable case details only in authorised, access-controlled reporting systems.
- Follow local privacy law, institutional policy and consent requirements.

## Quality checks
- Is the patient sufficiently identifiable within the authorised reporting system?
- Are age, sex and weight recorded where available and relevant?
- Are unknown values explicitly marked unknown/not reported?
- Has unnecessary identifying information been excluded from shared materials?

## Documentation principle
Do not infer or invent patient characteristics. Record the source and any uncertainty according to the applicable SOP.
