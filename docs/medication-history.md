# Module 03 — Suspected Medication and Medication History

## Purpose
Identify the product exposure that may be associated with the event and capture enough detail to evaluate it.

## Suspected product fields
| Field | What to record |
|---|---|
| Generic name | Active substance / INN where known |
| Brand name | Product brand, if applicable |
| Manufacturer/MAH | As required and available |
| Batch/lot number | Particularly relevant to biologicals, product defects or quality concerns |
| Dose | Amount per administration |
| Route | Oral, IV, topical, inhaled, etc. |
| Frequency | e.g. once daily, twice daily, TID |
| Indication | Reason for use |
| Start date | First exposure date |
| Stop date | Last exposure date, if stopped |
| Duration | Total treatment period |
| Action taken | Continued, reduced, interrupted or withdrawn |

## Concomitant products
Record other prescription medicines, OTC products, vaccines, herbal medicines, dietary supplements and relevant non-prescription exposures. Capture dates and doses when available, especially where interactions or alternative explanations are possible.

## Product-specific considerations
- Record the actual product and formulation where known.
- Distinguish prescribed dose from the dose actually taken.
- Document medication errors, overdose, misuse or off-label use when relevant.
- Do not assume manufacturer or batch details if they are unavailable.
- For combination products, document the ingredients according to the applicable reporting system.

## Quality checks
- Is at least one suspected/interacting product identified?
- Are dose, route, frequency, indication and exposure dates recorded where available?
- Are concomitant medicines included?
- Are missing values clearly identified rather than guessed?
