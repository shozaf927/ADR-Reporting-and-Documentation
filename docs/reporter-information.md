# Module 06 — Reporter Information and Report Governance

## Reporter information
Capture reporter details through the authorised reporting form or safety database, according to local requirements:
- Reporter name.
- Professional qualification or role.
- Institution/organisation.
- Professional address.
- Contact details.
- Signature, if required.
- Date the report was received or submitted.

## Confidentiality
Reporter identity and contact details must be handled according to the applicable reporting pathway and privacy rules. Do not place personal contact details in public GitHub examples.

## Causality assessment
Record the assessment method and conclusion, including the assessor and date where required. For individual case assessment, WHO–UMC categories may be used where appropriate. The assessment should consider chronology, known pharmacology, alternative causes, dechallenge and any available rechallenge information.

Do not treat the Bradford Hill considerations as a validated individual-patient scoring instrument. Do not claim that a suspected association is proven solely because the event followed drug exposure.

## Report lifecycle and follow-up
- Record initial receipt and report dates.
- Identify missing information.
- Request follow-up through approved channels.
- Preserve the original report and subsequent updates according to SOP.
- Maintain traceability of corrections and assessments.
- Submit within applicable regulatory timelines.

## Minimum ICSR validity elements
The four core elements are:
1. An identifiable patient.
2. An identifiable reporter.
3. At least one suspected or interacting medicinal product.
4. At least one adverse event/reaction or other relevant safety observation.

These minimum elements do not replace the need for a complete and clinically useful case.
